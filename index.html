Quadrun Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Quadrun ("the game") is a top-down arena game published by Burak Seker ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with data. It is short because the game does almost nothing with it: it is played entirely on your device, asks for no account, never connects to the internet and asks you for nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no server, the game contains no advertising or analytics software, and it does not even hold the permission to use the internet, so no data leaves your device. We cannot see who plays or how.

What stays on your device

The game keeps two numbers in its own private storage on the device: how many matches you have played and how many you have won. They are shown on the menu's record card and are never sent anywhere. The difficulty you pick is remembered only while the game is open.

Clearing that record is up to you: Android Settings → Apps → Quadrun → Storage → Clear data resets it, and uninstalling the game leaves nothing behind.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Permissions

The game asks for no dangerous permissions: no internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or storage. Vibration feedback goes through Android's haptic feedback and needs no permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address and the effective date above changes with it. If the game ever adds advertising or an online feature, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
